FDTDX 0.6.3 on the 2-D scenes (one cell thick, periodic in z):
  python fdtdx_bench.py --scene wg2d_45nm --recorder exact
fails while placing the mode detectors:
  fdtdx/objects/detectors/mode.py, in propagation_axis
  Exception: Invalid ModeOverlapDetector shape: (1, 24, 1)
(wg2d_15nm: (1, 44, 1); mm2d_45nm: (1, 132, 1); wg2d_5nm: (1, 396, 1)).
FDTDX's mode-overlap detector needs a plane at least two cells wide in both
transverse directions, so the 2-D scenes cannot be run without making the
domain thicker in z (which would multiply FDTDX's cost); they are not run.
